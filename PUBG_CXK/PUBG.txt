We are delighted to have produced a brand-new video for PUBG in collaboration with Cai Xukun (KUN). This film was made using a blend of traditional 3D CGI and cutting-edge AIGC. The car replacements and the overall layout and placement of the buildings come from a traditional 3D CGI pipeline rendered with C4D + Octane; the car chase scenes likewise combine 3D CGI (white-model layout) with AIGC technology. The overall result is a stylized long-form film, with roughly 80% of the entire piece generated and produced through AIGC.
